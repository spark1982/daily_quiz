Event Situation

You are to assume the role of marketing coordinator at Summit Wall Climbing Center, an indoor rock-climbing gym in Boise, Idaho. Most of the gym’s members are adults between 20 and 40. Next month, the gym will launch Junior Climbers, a new after-school program for children ages 8 to 13 that meets three afternoons a week. The program has room for 60 kids, and the owner hopes to fill it within six weeks.

The owner has set aside a promotion budget of $4,000 for the launch. She is thinking about spending the entire amount on one large billboard on the highway into town, because “everyone drives past it.” She is not sure that is the best choice, so she has asked you to recommend a promotional mix for Junior Climbers: which channels to use, how to divide the budget, and how the gym will know whether the promotion is working.

The owner (judge) will begin the role-play by asking for your recommendation in her office.

Judge's Question

“If we have only a few sign-ups after the first two weeks, what would you change, and how would we know which channel is not working?”

Task: In 150–250 words, write what you would say to the judge. Address all four performance indicators and answer the judge's question.

Model answer

Thank you for asking me, Ms. Owner. Promotion is the marketing function that tells customers about a product and persuades them to buy it, so our promotion must reach the people who decide: parents of kids ages 8 to 13. I recommend a promotional mix instead of one billboard. A highway billboard reaches every driver, but most of them do not have children in that age range. I would spend about $2,000 on digital advertising: Facebook and Instagram ads shown only to parents within 15 miles of the gym, with a 20-second video of kids reaching the top of a wall. I would use $1,000 on personal selling and public relations, with coaches visiting three nearby elementary schools and a story pitched to the local news. The last $1,000 would fund a sales promotion, a free “Try It Day” and a $25 early sign-up discount. To measure effectiveness, each channel gets its own tracking link or promo code, and the sign-up form asks how families heard about us. We will compare sign-ups and cost per sign-up for each channel every week. If we have only a few sign-ups after two weeks, those numbers will show which channel is weak. I would move that money into the strongest channel and add a second Try It Day. Thank you.
