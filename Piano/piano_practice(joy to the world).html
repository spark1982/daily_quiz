How to test
1. Starting from Middle C, play C, D, E, F, G slowly, one at a time.
2. If the big letter matches the key you played, it works.
3. The needle shows how in tune the note is. A little off is fine for practice.
4. If notes show up wrong often, move the device closer to the piano.
This screen checks one note at a time. Chords are checked in Song Practice.
