To strongly advise someone to use something

Right! “To strongly advise someone” is the heart of recommend. You can recommend more than things to use, too — a place to visit, a movie to watch, or something to do: The doctor recommended more sleep.

A big fight between two people

Meaning: a disagreement or struggle between people, groups, or ideas

Example: The two neighbors had a conflict about where the fence should go.

You wrote “A big fight between two people.” You are close — a fight can be a conflict. But a conflict does not have to be big, and it does not have to be a fight or only two people. It can be a disagreement between friends, a war between countries, or even a problem a character faces in a story. Try: “a disagreement or struggle between people or groups.”

colony (noun) Almost

Your answer

A group of people working together

Meaning: a group of people who settle in a new land but are still ruled by their home country; also, a group of animals that live together

Example: The Jamestown colony was started by English settlers in 1607.

You wrote “A group of people working together.” You have one piece — a colony is a group. The missing piece is where they are: people in a colony have moved to a new land and are still controlled by the country they came from, like the 13 colonies ruled by Britain. Animals can live in colonies too, like ants or bees. Try: “a group of people who settle in a new land ruled by their home country.”

dramatic (adjective) Got it!

Your answer

Exaggerating; making something a bigger deal than it already is

Meaning: showing big feelings or acting like something is a bigger deal than it is; also, sudden and exciting

Example: My little brother made a dramatic groan when Mom said it was bath time.

Yes! “Making something a bigger deal than it already is” is a real meaning of dramatic. It has another meaning, too: big and exciting, like a dramatic finish to the race.
