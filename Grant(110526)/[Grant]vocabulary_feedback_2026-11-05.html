reliable (adjective) Almost

Your answer

To trust someone

Meaning: able to be trusted or depended on to do the right thing every time

Example: My old bike is reliable — it has never broken down on the way to school.

You are close! Reliable is about trust. But your answer, “To trust someone,” describes the action of trusting. Reliable describes a person or thing that can be trusted. Try: “someone you can count on.” Look at the end of the word: -able means “can be” — rely + able = can be relied on.

contribute (verb) Not yet

Your answer

To make an agreement

Meaning: to give or add something (like money, time, or ideas) to help a group or a project

Example: Everyone in our class will contribute one food can to the food drive.

You wrote “To make an agreement.” That fits words like agree or promise, but contribute means giving your part to help. When each kid brings a snack for the class party, everyone contributes. Think of “chipping in.”

thrive (verb) Got it!

Your answer

To grow healthy

Meaning: to grow strong and healthy, or to do very well

Example: The tomato plants thrive in the warm, sunny garden.

Yes! “To grow healthy” is the heart of thrive. It can also mean a person or business is doing very well — a student can thrive at a new school.
