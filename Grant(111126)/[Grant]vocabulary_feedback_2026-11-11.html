grateful (adjective) Almost

Your answer

To be happy of something you get

Meaning: feeling thankful for something someone gave you or did for you

Example: Mia felt grateful when her neighbor helped carry the heavy boxes.

You are close! Being grateful does make you feel happy, but the key idea is thanks: you are grateful to someone for something. You can be happy about a sunny day, but you are grateful to the friend who shared their snack. Try: “feeling thankful for something you got.” (Small grammar tip: we say “happy about” or “thankful for,” not “happy of.”)
