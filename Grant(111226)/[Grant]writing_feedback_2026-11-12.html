The topic

Topic

Should summer vacation be longer?

Type of writing

Opinion — Tell what you think and give reasons.

How to write it

  1. Start by telling your opinion clearly: should summer vacation be longer, or should it stay the same?
  2. Give your first reason and an example from your own life.
  3. Give your second reason and another example.
  4. End with a closing sentence that repeats your opinion in new words.

Try to use these words

because  ·  exciting  ·  relaxing

Task: Write one paragraph (60–120 words). Use the plan above and try to use the three words.

What you did great

  • Your opinion comes first. “I think Summer vacation should be longer” tells the reader your side right away. That is exactly how an opinion paragraph should start.
  • You used signal words for your reasons. “My first reason” and “My second reason” work like road signs, so the reader always knows where they are.

Let’s make it even better

Here is everything that will make your paragraph stronger, starting with the most important. (Grammar is in its own section below.)

  1. 1. Add an example from your own life. The plan asked for a reason and an example from your own life. A real example makes a reason much more convincing. I added one — next time, use your own idea.

    Before: (no example after your first reason)
    After: “For example, last summer my family wanted to visit my cousins, but we ran out of time before school started.”

  2. 2. Explain your second reason. “more time to study and prepare for the next grade” is a good idea, but the reader may wonder how a vacation helps you study. Add one sentence that shows how.

    Before: (no sentence after your second reason)
    After: “A few extra relaxing weeks would let them review math and read more books without feeling rushed.”

  3. 3. Use only one because in a sentence. Your first sentence has two because parts, so it is hard to follow. Keep the reason that is most important.

    Before: “I think Summer vacation should be longer because then people can enjoy not having school for longer because some people say that they don’t have enough time to enjoy their Summer vacation.”
    After: “I think summer vacation should be longer because some people say they don’t have enough time to enjoy it.”

  4. 4. Close with new words. The plan asked you to repeat your opinion in new words. Your last sentence repeats your first sentence almost word for word. Start with a closing signal like In short and say it a new way.

    Before: “I think Summer vacation should be longer people then people can have more time to enjoy not having any school and can have more fun.”
    After: “In short, a longer summer would give everyone more time to have fun and get ready to learn.”

  5. 5. Use exact words instead of general ones. “have more time to plan vacations” is fine, but plan exciting trips paints a clearer picture and uses one of your helper words.

    Before: “people can have more time to plan vacations or have friends over.”
    After: “a longer break gives families more time to plan exciting trips or have friends over.”

Grammar fixes

  1. 1. Seasons start with a small letter. Names of seasons (spring, summer, fall, winter) are not proper nouns, so they are not capitalized unless they begin a sentence.

    Before: “Summer vacation”
    After: “summer vacation”

  2. 2. Say “My reason is that,” not “is because” or “is so.” After My reason is, use that to introduce the reason.

    Before: “My first reason is because then people can have…” / “My second reason is so then people can have…”
    After: “My first reason is that people can have…” / “My second reason is that people can have…”

  3. 3. Read your sentence aloud to catch repeated words. In your last sentence, a joining word is missing and people is written twice, so it doesn’t make sense.

    Before: “should be longer people then people can have”
    After: “should be longer so that people can have”

Did you use the three words?

  • because — Yes! “…should be longer because then people can enjoy…”
  • exciting — Not yet. You could say “plan exciting trips”.
  • relaxing — Not yet. You could say “a few extra relaxing weeks”.
