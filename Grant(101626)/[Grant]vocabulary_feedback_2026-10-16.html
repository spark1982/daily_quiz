You wrote “To wait and see what happens.” You are close: an outcome is “what happens” — but it is the result itself, not the waiting. Also, outcome is a noun (a thing), so its meaning should not start with “To.” Try: “the result of something.”

You understood the big idea: none of that kind exist anymore. Even better wording: “when every one of a kind of animal has died out.” (Breed is used for kinds of dogs, cats, or farm animals; for wild animals, say species or kind.)
