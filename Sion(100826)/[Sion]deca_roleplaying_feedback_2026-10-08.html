Event Situation

You are to assume the role of customer service manager at Stride Lab, an online company in Portland, Oregon, that sells custom-designed basketball shoes. Customers choose their own colors and add names or numbers, and each pair costs $140. Most orders ship in 3 weeks.

Five weeks ago, the head coach of the Lincoln High School girls’ basketball team ordered 14 custom pairs in the team’s colors, blue and gold, for a total of $1,960. The coach told Stride Lab the shoes were needed for the season opener this Friday. Yesterday the box finally arrived, but all 14 pairs are red and gold, and six players’ numbers are misspelled or wrong. The coach has posted an angry review online and called the company demanding a full refund. Your production team says correct shoes can be made in 6 business days, or in 3 business days with $400 of rush shipping and overtime.

The coach (judge) will begin the role-play by telling you, on a video call, exactly how upset the team is.

Judge's Question

“Why should I ever trust Stride Lab again, and what are you going to do so my players aren’t embarrassed on Friday?”

Task: In 150–250 words, write what you would say to the judge. Address all four performance indicators and answer the judge's question.

Your answer

I am so sorry that this has happened. In order to try to preserve a positive customer relation with you, I want to assure you that we are going to do everything in our power to make things right for you. We can absolutely get a full refund of $1,960 for you, and I will get that sorted out as soon as possible. You must feel very frustrated right now, and rightfully so. The shoes were wrong and the wait was two weeks longer than usual. We deeply apologize again for this. Stride Lab always tries to understand frustrated customers and resolve their issues quickly and effectively. So, we will also immediately get started on the production of a new, free order of the 14 shoes for you, which we will ensure are correct this time to resolve this conflict. Unfortunately, the soonest we can usually get these to you is in six business days. However, as some compensation for the stress we have caused you, we are going to also completely cover the $400 cost of rush shipping and overtime it takes to get this new order to you as soon as physically possible: in three business days. This should ensure that your players get their new, correct shoes in time for their season opener. I hope our services can prove that we truly care about your situation and can re-earn some trust from you. Thank you, and please let us know if you have any more concerns.

What you did well

  • You handled the coach’s anger the right way. “You must feel very frustrated right now, and rightfully so” shows empathy before any explanation, which is exactly how to calm a difficult customer.
  • You used the options in the case. Choosing the 3-day rush and having Stride Lab pay the $400 shows you read the situation carefully and put the team’s deadline first.
  • Your length and tone were right. At 249 words, your answer was complete, polite, and easy to follow from start to finish.

What to improve

  • Make the fix fair to both sides. Instead of “We can absolutely get a full refund of $1,960 for you” plus free shoes, try: “We will remake all 14 pairs at no cost, pay the $400 rush ourselves, and take 25% off your next team order.”
  • Explain the PI, don’t just name it. Instead of “In order to try to preserve a positive customer relation with you,” try: “Positive customer relations mean customers trust us enough to come back and recommend us. Right now we have broken that trust, so I want to earn it back.”
  • Answer “why trust us” with a reason. Instead of “I hope our services can prove that we truly care,” try: “From now on, every team order is checked by a second person, and you will approve a photo of the shoes before we ship.”
  • Check the deadline. Instead of “This should ensure that your players get their new, correct shoes in time,” try: “If we start today, the shoes arrive Thursday; I will send you tracking updates each morning and call you the moment they ship.”
  • Apologize once, strongly. Cut “We deeply apologize again for this” and use the space for one creative extra, like free team socks for the season opener.

What the judge was looking for

  • PI 1: Positive customer relations build loyalty, repeat orders, and good word of mouth. One angry online review can scare away other coaches, so fixing this relationship matters beyond one order.
  • PI 2: Listen, let the customer speak, acknowledge feelings, apologize, and stay calm and specific without blaming.
  • PI 3: Offer a solution that solves the real problem (correct shoes by Friday) and is fair to the business, then confirm the customer agrees.
  • PI 4: Show service through communication: direct contact person, photo approval, tracking updates, and a follow-up call after the game. Asking the coach to update the review once things are fixed is also reasonable.
  • Judge’s question: Trust comes from a specific change (a quality check on every custom order) plus a guaranteed plan for Friday.

Model answer

Coach, thank you for telling me directly. You ordered blue and gold shoes for Friday’s opener and received red ones with six wrong names and numbers. That is our mistake, and I am truly sorry.

Here is what we will do. We will remake all 14 pairs today at no cost, and Stride Lab will pay the $400 for rush production and shipping, so the shoes arrive in three business days, by Thursday. Before we ship, I will text you a photo of every pair so you can approve the colors and numbers. You will have my direct phone number, and I will send tracking updates each morning. To thank you for your patience, we will also take 25% off your next team order and send free team socks for the opener.

Why trust us again? Because good customer relations mean earning your next order, not just this one. Starting today, every team order is checked by a second employee before it leaves our building.

After Friday, I will call to make sure everything went well. If you are happy then, we would be grateful if you updated your review.

The model chooses free replacement plus a discount instead of a full refund, so the solution is generous but still fair to the business.
