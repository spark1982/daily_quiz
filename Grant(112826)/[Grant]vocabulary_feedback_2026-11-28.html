Almost! “Make sure” is the right idea, but you verify that something is true or correct, not that it is good. A fact can be verified even if it is bad news.

Your answer

To be optional

Meaning: first or most important; main

Example: Our primary goal is to finish the science project by Friday.

Not quite. Optional means you can choose to do it or not. Primary means the opposite kind of thing: the first or most important one, like a primary color or a primary reason.
