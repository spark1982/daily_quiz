You wrote “Something that happens to a thing.” That sounds like the word effect (a result — what happens because of something). Effective is a describing word: something is effective when it works well. An effective plan works; an effective medicine helps you get better.

You wrote “to say something again.” That meaning belongs to words like repeat or restate. Classify is about sorting: you put things into classes, or groups. Look inside the word — classify has class in it, and a class is a group.

Words to practice

  • category (noun) — a group of things that are alike
  • effective (adjective) — working well; doing what it should
  • classify (verb) — to sort things into groups

Memory tip: Hidden words help! Classify has class (a group) inside it, and effective means something has a good effect — it works.
