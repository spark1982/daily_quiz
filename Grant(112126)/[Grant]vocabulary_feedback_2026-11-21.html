Your answer

To check again to make sure something is right

Meaning: to make certain that something happens or is true

Example: Wear your helmet to ensure that you stay safe.

You are close — the words “make sure” are the heart of it! But ensure does not mean checking again (that is verify or double-check). To ensure means to do something so that a result will happen: you lock the door to ensure no one gets in.

Words to practice

ensure — Memory tip: ensure = make it sure. You ensure something before it happens (pack an umbrella to ensure you stay dry). You verify something after, by checking.
