Your answer

To be able to control the thing

Meaning: to put or keep someone or something apart, away from others

Example: The nurse isolated the sick student so the others would not catch the flu.

You wrote “To be able to control the thing.” That sounds more like control or manage. To isolate is to separate something so it is alone. Memory trick: an island is land all alone in the water, and isolate starts almost the same way. Try: “to keep something apart from others.”

Right! That is the most common meaning. Remember the second one too: to use up all of something.
